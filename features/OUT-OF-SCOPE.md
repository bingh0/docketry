# Out of scope — the docket lint

The fence for the docket grammar and its lint, scoped 2026-09-04 as a
feature inside the scope plugin. Entries carry their ratification dates;
a reaffirmation or reversal updates the date in place. Every entry below
is also a resolution in `DOCKET.md`, cited by docket id.

## Declined

- **Coverage by inference.** Siblings are never matched from touches
  links or trigger text; a wanted entry names its unwanted entries or
  says none with a reason. Inference masked two of thirteen known
  omissions in the field test. Guarded by: "a wanted entry with a
  trigger and no sib line is a coverage finding" (D92, D14, 2026-09-04)
- **Open vocabularies and a normalizing pass.** Prose lives only inside
  slots; every other field is a closed set, and a normalizer would be a
  second grammar nobody specified. Guarded by: "a provenance tag outside
  the four refuses the docket naming the line", "a resolution kind outside
  the fixed set refuses the docket" (D93, D15, 2026-09-04)
- **Dates updated in place, and undated entries.** The docket is
  append-only with typed relations; in-place rewrites lose history under
  rule 7's touches, and an undated entry cannot be walked. Guarded by:
  "an undated entry is a form finding" (D94, D16, 2026-09-04)
- **Gherkin keywords as slot keywords.** A docket must never be
  mistakable for a feature file. Guarded by: "a Gherkin keyword used as
  a slot keyword refuses the docket" (D95, D18, 2026-09-04)
- **A regex heuristic for quantities.** Ruled in, then reversed the same
  day: quantities are syntax, a node kind; a bare digit run is a lexical
  near-miss. Guarded by: "a bare digit run outside braces is a lexical
  near-miss finding" (D96, D19 reversed by D20, 2026-09-04)
- **The lint writing or remembering.** No last-run record, no
  normalizing in place. A second historian beside the journal is the
  self-grounding hazard the audit fence closed. Guarded by: "the lint
  leaves the docket and deliverables byte-identical", "two consecutive
  runs over identical inputs report identically" (D97, D29, 2026-09-04)
- **An empty docket reported as clean.** Nothing to lint is a
  could-not-run refusal, never exit zero. Guarded by: "nothing to lint
  exits two naming the cause" (D98, D26, 2026-09-04)
- **Roll-ups or warnings at size.** Size is not a behaviour; roll-up is
  caller-side. Guarded by: "a docket of ten thousand entries lists every
  finding in full" (D99, D27, 2026-09-04)
- **Flagging directive text in slot prose.** A heuristic over meaning;
  the steering hazard belongs to prose-reading consumers. Guarded by:
  "slot text addressed to the reader changes nothing in the report"
  (D100, D36, 2026-09-04)
- **Gating by default, and gating left entirely to the consumer.**
  Strict is an opt-in flag; default reports and exits zero. Guarded by:
  "default mode exits zero with findings present", "strict mode exits
  one with the same findings" (D101, D10, 2026-09-04)
- **A scaffold command.** Findings state their fix instead. (D102, D33,
  2026-09-04)
- **Refusing the whole lint when gnt is absent.** Five layers run on the
  docket alone. Guarded by: "with a corpus given and no gnt parser the
  run exits two naming the parser" (D103, D35, 2026-09-04)
- **A layer-plugin interface for future grammars.** An interface
  designed before its second user exists. (D104, D39, 2026-09-04)
- **A threshold finding on provenance share.** The distribution is
  shown, never judged. Guarded by: "a docket worded entirely by the
  interviewer and ratified reports no provenance finding" (D105, D44,
  2026-09-04)
- **The fence as the home for "no failure case" claims.** Replaced by
  `sib: none -- <reason>` inline, counted, with vacuous reasons a form
  finding. Guarded by: "sib none with a vacuous reason is a form
  finding" (D106, D45, 2026-09-04)

## Deferred — each with its reopening condition

- **binding-side-lint.** If the docket ever becomes a runner with worlds
  and step definitions, a linter on the binding side must exist too.
  Reopens on that day. (D3, 2026-09-04)
- **cadence.** Whether the mid-interview lint runs per question, per
  checkpoint, or per feature group. Decided during build; the lint is
  stateless so any cadence works. (D7, 2026-09-04)
- **journal corroboration.** Checking a `[V]` tag against the
  installation's journal. Reopens when a journal-reading consumer
  exists. (D86 ratified by D89, 2026-09-05)
- **rejected-options-as-entries.** The resolution set carries no
  `road-not-taken` kind; this first docket records rejected options as
  comments and lists them here. Whether every rejected option becomes
  its own entry, the cino:decision catch inside the grammar, with the
  kind re-admitted by a dated amendment to D15, reopens on the first
  re-litigated ruling a docket-reading agent could have prevented.
  (D55 ratified by D61, amended by D63, 2026-09-05)
- **a-second-profile.** The aBDD destination table is one profile over
  the core; the kind set becomes pluggable when a second profile
  arrives, the general interview register. (D144, 2026-09-06)
- **pinned-citations.** Hash-pinned citations — the id, a hyphen, and
  the first six hex of the cited chain's effective entry hash on every
  citation outside the docket; a stale pin a traceability finding, an
  unpinned citation counted and refused under strict. Drafted as D224
  after the INCOSE walk and lifted unratified to
  `docs/lifted-drafts-20260909.md`. Reopens on the first stale citation
  a scoped corpus shows after an amendment; the hackathon corpora of
  2026-10-17 are the first field data. Until then a citing surface is
  re-read by hand after each amendment, as the touches line directs.
  (D233, 2026-10-05)
- **ratifying-the-never-inferred.** A `ratifies` entry whose target was
  never tagged inferred is a consistency case not yet ruled; held at
  handoff. (D56 ratified by D62, 2026-09-05)

## Named assumptions

- **provenance-uncorroborated.** A `[V]` tag is the interviewer's claim
  about the visionary; no lint can decide it. (D32, ratified D48,
  2026-09-04)
- **The field-test catch rate rests on one run.** Four to six of
  thirteen known omissions, from one retrospective re-transcription of
  the /audit interview. The first live run is the second data point.
  (D87 ratified by D90, 2026-09-05)

- **sibs-and-serves-are-claims.** A sib-none reason, a sibling, a wanted
  parent, and a serves edge are tokens the lint counts, never judges;
  whether they are true is the audit skill's checklist. (D158,
  2026-09-06)
- **the-deferred-escape.** Deferring is the legal way out of any awkward
  behaviour; it lands here, and the rendered entry carries the trigger
  as its reopening condition. (D159, 2026-09-06)

## Out of reach by construction — stated so nobody expects it

- Quantity and list coverage is opt-in by bracing; a number or a list
  written as plain words is content and stays invisible by rule. (D121)

- A scenario weakened after its ruling's date with no amending entry is
  temporal and gherkin-trace's lane; the docket now supplies the date per
  tag, which it never had. (D24)
- An unbraced list, or a second failure case inside a slot, is content
  and judgment's lane; a braced list is spread like a quantity. (D24,
  D121)

- Editor integration — highlighting, a language server, in-editor
  squiggles — is nobody's lane here; a finding names its line and its
  one correct form, and any editor's problems pane can show that from
  the machine form. (D33, D41)
